Negatively charged particles.
Occupy the conduction band.
(b) Holes
Positive charge carriers created when electrons leave the valence band.
Both electrons and holes contribute to current flow.
Intrinsic Semiconductor
An intrinsic semiconductor is pure and contains equal numbers of electrons and holes.
Characteristics:
No impurity atoms.
Low conductivity.
Conductivity increases with temperature.
Extrinsic Semiconductor
Conductivity is increased by adding impurities (doping).
N-type Semiconductor
Formed by adding pentavalent impurities.
Majority carriers: Electrons.
Minority carriers: Holes.
Examples of dopants:
Phosphorus
Arsenic
Antimony
P-type Semiconductor
Produced by adding trivalent impurities.
Majority carriers:
Holes
