(c) Field Emission
Produced by applying a very strong electric field.
The field pulls electrons out of the metal.
Applications
Electron microscopes
Flat-panel displays
(d) Secondary Emission
Occurs when high-energy electrons strike a metal surface, causing additional electrons to be released.
Applications
Electron multipliers
Photomultiplier tubes
